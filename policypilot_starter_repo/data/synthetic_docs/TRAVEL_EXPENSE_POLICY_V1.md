## 1. Purpose
This policy explains reimbursable business travel and expense rules.

## 2. Air Travel
Economy airfare is reimbursable. Premium economy requires director approval for flights longer than 6 hours. Business class is not reimbursable.

## 3. Hotels
Hotel costs up to 225 CAD per night before taxes are reimbursable in major Canadian cities. Higher rates require pre-approval.

## 4. Meals
Daily meal reimbursement caps are 20 CAD for breakfast, 25 CAD for lunch, and 45 CAD for dinner.

## 5. Ground Transport
Taxi, rideshare, public transit, and mileage are reimbursable when reasonable and business-related.

## 6. Receipt Rules
Receipts are required for all expenses above 25 CAD.

## 7. Submission Timeline
Expense claims must be submitted within 20 calendar days after travel ends.
