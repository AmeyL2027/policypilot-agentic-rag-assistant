## 1. Intake
All complaints must be logged in the case management system within 1 business day.

## 2. Severity levels
- Low: service issue with no financial impact
- Medium: repeat service issue or moderate delay
- High: financial harm, privacy concern, or regulatory risk

## 3. Escalation
High-severity complaints must be escalated to Compliance on the same business day.

## 4. Response timelines
Initial acknowledgement must be sent within 2 business days. Final resolution target is 10 business days for low/medium severity and 5 business days for high severity.
