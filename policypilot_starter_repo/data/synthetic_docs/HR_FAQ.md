## Q: When do I complete mandatory training?
New hires must complete mandatory training during their first week, and some annual compliance training may apply later.

## Q: Can I use a personal laptop?
No. Company work involving internal systems must use approved company-managed devices unless an exception is formally approved.

## Q: How many vacation days do I receive?
Check the latest handbook version because vacation entitlements changed in the 2025 handbook update.
