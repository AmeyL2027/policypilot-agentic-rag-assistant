## 1. Access request types
Requests may include laptop issuance, email setup, VPN access, CRM access, analytics access, and shared drive access.

## 2. Approval requirements
Standard access requires manager approval. Access to production systems requires manager approval and security approval. Admin privileges require director approval.

## 3. Provisioning timelines
- Email and chat: within 1 business day
- VPN: within 2 business days
- Shared drive: within 2 business days
- Production system access: within 3 business days after approval

## 4. Audit logging
All privileged access approvals must be logged for audit review.
