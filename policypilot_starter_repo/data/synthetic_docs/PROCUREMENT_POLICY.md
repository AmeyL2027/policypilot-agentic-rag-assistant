## 1. Purpose
This policy governs purchasing of goods and services.

## 2. Competitive quotes
- Purchases below 1,000 CAD do not require competitive quotes.
- Purchases from 1,000 CAD to 9,999.99 CAD require one written quote.
- Purchases from 10,000 CAD to 49,999.99 CAD require at least three written quotes.
- Purchases of 50,000 CAD or more require a formal procurement process.

## 3. Vendor onboarding
New vendors must complete tax and banking verification before payment setup.

## 4. Conflict of interest
Employees involved in vendor selection must disclose any conflict of interest.
