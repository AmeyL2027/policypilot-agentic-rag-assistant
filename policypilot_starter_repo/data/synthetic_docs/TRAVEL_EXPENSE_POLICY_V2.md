## 1. Purpose
This policy explains reimbursable business travel and expense rules.

## 2. Air Travel
Economy airfare is reimbursable. Premium economy requires director approval for flights longer than 5 hours. Business class remains non-reimbursable unless explicitly approved by the vice president for medical or operational reasons.

## 3. Hotels
Hotel costs up to 250 CAD per night before taxes are reimbursable in major Canadian cities. Higher rates require written pre-approval.

## 4. Meals
Daily meal reimbursement caps are 22 CAD for breakfast, 28 CAD for lunch, and 50 CAD for dinner.

## 5. Ground Transport
Taxi, rideshare, public transit, mileage, and parking are reimbursable when reasonable and business-related.

## 6. Receipt Rules
Receipts are required for all expenses above 20 CAD.

## 7. Submission Timeline
Expense claims must be submitted within 15 calendar days after travel ends.
