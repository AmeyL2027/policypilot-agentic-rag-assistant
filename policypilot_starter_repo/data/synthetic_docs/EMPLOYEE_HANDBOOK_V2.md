## 1. Purpose
This handbook describes the core employment practices of MapleSure Operations.

## 2. Work Hours
Standard work hours are 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM local time, Monday to Friday. Managers may approve flexible schedules based on business coverage requirements.

## 3. Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to four days per week with manager approval. Fully remote roles require director approval and annual review.

## 4. Leave
Full-time employees receive 18 vacation days annually. Sick leave is accrued at 1.25 days per month. Up to two wellness days may be used each year without medical documentation.

## 5. Code of Conduct
Employees must protect confidential information, avoid harassment and discrimination, complete annual compliance training, and report policy breaches promptly.

## 6. Equipment
Company-issued laptops and approved endpoint protection must be used for all work involving internal systems. Personal devices may not store customer data unless specifically approved through mobile device management.

## 7. Training
All new hires must complete information-security training, workplace conduct training, and privacy training within 7 business days of joining.
