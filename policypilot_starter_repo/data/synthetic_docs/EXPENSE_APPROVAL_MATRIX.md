## Approval thresholds
- Up to 250 CAD: employee's direct manager
- 250.01 to 1,000 CAD: department manager
- 1,000.01 to 5,000 CAD: director
- Above 5,000 CAD: vice president

## Special rules
- International travel always requires director approval.
- Software subscriptions longer than 12 months require procurement review.
- Any exception to policy requires written justification and finance approval.
