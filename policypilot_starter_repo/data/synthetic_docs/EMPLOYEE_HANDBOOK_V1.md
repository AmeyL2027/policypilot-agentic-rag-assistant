## 1. Purpose
This handbook describes the core employment practices of MapleSure Operations.

## 2. Work Hours
Standard work hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time, Monday to Friday. Managers may approve flexible schedules based on team needs.

## 3. Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Fully remote roles require director approval and must be documented in the employee record.

## 4. Leave
Full-time employees receive 15 vacation days annually. Sick leave is accrued at 1.25 days per month. Personal emergency leave may be approved by HR for exceptional circumstances.

## 5. Code of Conduct
Employees must protect confidential information, avoid harassment and discrimination, and follow all information-security requirements.

## 6. Equipment
Company-issued laptops must be used for all work involving internal systems. Personal devices may not store customer data.

## 7. Training
All new hires must complete information-security training and workplace conduct training within 10 business days of joining.
