# Public document sources for PolicyPilot

Download **6–8** of these official public documents and put them in `data/public_docs/`.

## Recommended starter set
1. **Employee Handbook — University of North Georgia**  
   https://ung.edu/human-resources/_uploads/files/Employee-Handbook.pdf
2. **Employee Handbook — Fashion Institute of Technology**  
   https://www.fitnyc.edu/documents/hr/hr-employee-handbook.pdf
3. **Acceptable Use Policy — Illinois Department of Innovation & Technology**  
   https://doit.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/doit/documents/support/policies/2022/acceptable-use-policy.pdf
4. **Digital Accessibility Policy — Washington Technology Solutions**  
   https://watech.wa.gov/sites/default/files/2025-01/USER%20-01%20Digital%20Accessibility%20Policy.pdf
5. **Guidance on Web Accessibility and the ADA — U.S. Department of Justice**  
   https://www.ada.gov/assets/pdfs/web-guidance.pdf
6. **Town of Colonie Purchasing Policy and Procedures**  
   https://www.townofcolonie.gov/departments/genservices/documents/TownProcurementPoliciesandProcedures.pdf
7. **Purchasing Policies and Procedures — City of South San Francisco**  
   https://www.ssfca.gov/files/assets/public/v/1/finance/documents/no.-1-purchasing-policy.pdf
8. **Policy for the Procurement of Goods and Services — LACERA**  
   https://www.lacera.gov/sites/default/files/assets/documents/board/Governing%20Documents/General%20Policies/Purchasing_Policy_Goods_Services.pdf

## Suggested metadata fields when you ingest these
- `title`
- `source_type` = `public`
- `doc_type` = `policy`, `handbook`, `guidance`, `faq`, `sop`
- `department`
- `version`
- `effective_date`
- `url`

## Tip
Pick documents from **different types** so your retriever sees varied layouts: handbooks, accessibility guidance, IT policy, and procurement policy.
